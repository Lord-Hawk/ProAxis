Get started

Install in under a minute

ProMandi installs from the browser, with no app store needed. Open lord-hawk.github.io/ProMandi on the phone or computer it will be used on.

Android Chrome

  1. Open the app link in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the gold Install as Mobile App button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Install in the box that appears.
  4. ProMandi appears on your home screen and in your app list.

No box? Tap ⋮ in Chrome, then Install app.

iPhone & iPad Safari

  1. Open the app link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. ProMandi appears on your home screen.

Installing does not work from Chrome on iPhone; use Safari.

Laptop & PC Chrome or Edge

  1. Open the app link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. ProMandi opens in its own window, with a desktop shortcut.

Always open ProMandi from its icon, and allow location when asked.

Register your device, once

Head of Operations
Share your records Google Sheet with ProMandi as Editor, paste its link in the form, and enter the OTP sent to the ProMandi owner. Your email is required.
Manager, Accountant or DSO
Enter your First Name and Mobile No., then your Head of Operations' name and email exactly as registered. The OTP goes to your Head; type it in to finish. A Manager also enters a one-word Management Role, such as Sales.
After that
"This device has been registered" appears. Tap Next and your home screen opens with the options for your role.
