What it does

Built around how a beat actually runs

ProMandi follows the markets, counters and products already in your records. Your team picks from lists instead of typing, so the sheet stays clean.

Log Visits

Beat-based visit logging

Today's beat and its markets load automatically. Pick the market and counter, add the order with payment terms, the payment received, remarks and interest in cooler. Out of Beat visits work too, with every market listed.

Orders

Products in four taps

Type, Item, Sub-Item and Colour drop down one after another, and the rate fills in from your price history. The order total and quantity are worked out as you go.

Share

A summary ready for WhatsApp

After every visit or payment, ProMandi writes a neat summary of the counter, products, total, terms and payments. Copy it or share it straight to the customer or your manager.

Beat Details

Targets and pending payments

See each market's monthly target against this month's orders, with the achieved and pending percentage. Switch to Payments Pending to see which counters in the beat still owe money.

Counter Search

A counter's whole history

Every visit, order, invoice and payment for one counter, all time, with billed, received and pending totals and due dates. It opens instantly because the data is already on the phone.

Accounts

Inward and outward invoices

The accountant logs inward invoices with cash discount, raises outward invoices numbered by financial year, and edits or cancels a previous invoice when something changes.

Offline

Works without signal

Entries are saved on the device first and upload by themselves when the connection is back. The app opens and runs offline for up to three days.

Control

You decide who uses it

Every device registers with an OTP approved by its Head of Operations. Change a person's position or block a device from the Users sheet, and the app follows within seconds.

Get started

Install in under a minute

ProMandi installs from the browser, with no app store needed. Open lord-hawk.github.io/ProMandi on the phone or computer it will be used on.

Android Chrome

  1. Open the app link in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the gold Install as Mobile App button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Install in the box that appears.
  4. ProMandi appears on your home screen and in your app list.

No box? Tap ⋮ in Chrome, then Install app.

iPhone & iPad Safari

  1. Open the app link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. ProMandi appears on your home screen.

Installing does not work from Chrome on iPhone; use Safari.

Laptop & PC Chrome or Edge

  1. Open the app link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. ProMandi opens in its own window, with a desktop shortcut.

Always open ProMandi from its icon, and allow location when asked.

Register your device, once

Head of Operations
Share your records Google Sheet with ProMandi as Editor, paste its link in the form, and enter the OTP sent to the ProMandi owner. Your email is required.
Manager, Accountant or DSO
Enter your First Name and Mobile No., then your Head of Operations' name and email exactly as registered. The OTP goes to your Head; type it in to finish. A Manager also enters a one-word Management Role, such as Sales.
After that
"This device has been registered" appears. Tap Next and your home screen opens with the options for your role.
